## Line by Line 0.7.0

- **Highlights:** press `Ctrl+Alt+.` to highlight the line the band is on, like a highlighter pen; press it again to remove it. Highlights stay as you move on, so you can see at a glance which lines are done. They're remembered per file.
- **Settings → Highlights:**
  - **Colour:** green by default, with yellow, blue, pink and orange presets or any custom colour, plus how strong it is.
  - **What:** the whole line (default) or just the value Value Grab would copy.
  - **Which lines:** the top line of the band (default) or every line in it.
  - **Highlight lines automatically** as the band moves past them (off by default).
  - **Save highlights into the PDF file** (off by default) so Acrobat, Edge and other viewers show them too. Off means the file is never changed.
  - **Clear all highlights** for the open document.
- Fixed: opening Settings could quietly reset a slider to its lowest value.
